# Cells from the inflow wall: 1D moving Shu-Osher on LUNA's domain, 2026-11-06

voronoi-cpp #25, luna-cpp #3. Tags: **[M]** measured, **[D]** derived, **[I]** inference, not tested.

## The problem

On a moving mesh a fixed wall with inflow needs new generators as the gas moves away from it. Without them the first cell
stretches by v t = 4.7 on a line of length 10, independent of N: L1 rho stalls at 0.037 and the left-going waves at x = -2.5,
-1.5, -0.5 are lost (stage1_1d_2026-11-04.md).

## What was built

- **Library** (`sorted_mesh.hpp`, `Mesh<1>`): `insert(points)`, `remove(ids)`, `reserve(n)`, and `Changes`, the report: ids new or
  gone, deferred requests (-1), the neighbours of each on the line or the wall, the lengths overlapped, and the old volumes. A
  binary search per request and one pass over the line, no sort. Arrays grow by a quarter when full. Ids stay dense, the
  last ids filling holes (`Changes::moved`): in 1D a rename costs a sort at most, and only if keys tie. The design note's holes
  (11.7) were for 3D. The library knows nothing of inflow.
- **Hydro** (`finite_volume_1d.hpp`): a wall that is open (inflow or zero gradient), through which gas enters, adds a generator.
  `remove()` is not used: at an outflow end nothing here needs it, as no test has points reach an open wall in time.
  Limitation: gas leaving through an open wall piles points at the wall; unchanged by this work, and not tested.

## The inflow state must come from the wall, not the end cell

First ladder with LUNA's zero-gradient wall (job 31951835): the totals missed the budget through the walls by 1.1e-11 at N = 1024, up 4x
per doubling, to **1.8e-7 at 2^17**, in rho, momentum and energy alike and of one sign **[M]**. With a fixed inflow state the same
code gives 3e-15 to 4e-14 **[M]**, so nothing leaks: conservation held, and the end cell's state crept up.

Mechanism **[I]**, the size fits within a factor 3 **[D]**: the end cell's volume comes from snapped keys, low by half a grid step on average,
so its density rises by about (step / 2) / (2 delta) over one growth from delta to 2 delta. Each split passes that on, and about N / 2 splits
follow, each of size proportional to N, hence N^2: 4.4e-7 estimated at 2^17 against 1.8e-7. The effect on L1 is below the digits
printed.

So moving Shu-Osher takes `Wall::inflow` holding LEFT; static stays on LUNA's zero-gradient wall, which equals LUNA. A subsonic inflow (Titarev-Toro)
must stay zero gradient to let waves out, and keeps this drift.

## The second stall

rho falls at order 1 to 2^15, then 0.79, then 0.48 **[D]**; v and P stay at order 1.0 to the top. The ratio to LUNA rises from 0.53 to 0.81, all of it
in x > 0 (0.41 to 0.75), the gas with entropy structure that started on the line. This fits the frozen-entropy stall of the notes; reported and not chased.
