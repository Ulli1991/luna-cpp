Branch tests-1d. [M] measured, [I] inference. Runs: /raven/u/uli/voronoi-cpp/build-1dtests/runs (scripts/ladder_1d_a100.sh, A100s),

## Static equals LUNA [M]

On a uniform line our static scheme is LUNA's: L1 of every test, MC, minmod and van Leer, equals LUNA's within 1e-9 to 1e-14 at
N <= 2048. Rounding grows with N: la-slow 1e-3 at 2^16 (same steps, max state difference 1.3e-3; [I] amplified by the slow shock's
start-up oscillations), pure shocks up to 3e-5, the linear waves at their floor. Where LUNA crashes ours runs: rho = 0 (LA vacuum,
LUNA's NaN dt at t = 0) and Noh at N = 256 (LUNA NaN after 36 steps, all limiters).

## #23 fixed (2026-11-10, branch fix-23) [M]

Cause: the face velocity was the mean of the point velocities while the volumes came from the rounded positions (x + dt w
rounds to ulp(x)), so V^n+1 - V^n and dt (w_R - w_L) differed by a random walk of rounding; at amplitude 1e-6 that is the
cell-to-cell noise. Measured on sound-right (L1 of rho in LUNA's norm): regularisation off, a fixed dt: no change; points
still: no growth; points at a uniform 0.5: growth (rounding alone); L1 ~ (dV/V) / (2 eps) at every N. Fix: the kernel snaps
the moved points to keys first and a face's velocity is its move on the grid over dt; the moving step is MUSCL-Hancock on
both meshes. Sound right moving, before -> after: 2.37e-6 -> 7.9e-7 (4096), 2.70e-5 -> 4.95e-8 (16384), 5.84e-5 -> 3.19e-8
(32768), 1.21e-4 -> 2.4e-8 (65536), 4.46e-4 -> 1.37e-7 (131072, the rounding floor; LUNA 5.9e-8). Entropy moving stays at
rounding, 7e-10 to 2.6e-7 (static 9.3e-7 at 131072). Shocks: within 10 % except single rungs (pure shocks at 32768-131072,
LeBlanc 1024, near-vacuum v), mixed sign. la-streams moving 65536 now needs 778 s > the 13-minute job (714 s before): missing.
