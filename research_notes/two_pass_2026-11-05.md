# Two passes on moving 3D meshes: what they cost, 2026-11-05

The owner's decision: two passes are the default for moving meshes at order 1; `--integrator=one-pass` keeps the old step
and its bits. Static runs and order 0 are unchanged. Read against AREPO next generation (`src/main/run.cc:284, 165`,
`src/mesh/mesh_fluxes.cc:620-712`); ported from the diagnosis of the 3D order loss in voronoi-cpp, branch dim-1d-diag
(`research_notes/1d_moving_diagnosis_2026-11-05.md`, notes 19, 23): integrator only, no flattening and no diagnosis switches.

All runs: one A100 40 GB on ravg1002, double, jittered, one job per comparison, HLLC, Courant 0.3. Clock by job (`clock-<job>.csv`):
1305 MHz: speed 31925156, Sedov 31925157, vortex 31925337. 1095 MHz: mixed 31925336, simple 31925412, tube 31925413,
memory options 31925454. Compare within a job, not across.

## The cost

| Moving Sedov, jittered, to t_end | 128^3 one | 128^3 two | 256^3 one | 256^3 two |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M cell updates/s, whole step, median | 80.0 | 63.5 | 87.9 | 67.5 |
| hydro, ms per step (fluxes + update included) | 15.3 | 22.1 | 120.9 | 178.4 |
| mesh, ms per step (drift, update, cell lists) | 10.9 | 10.9 | 69.9 | 70.0 |
| steps | 348 | 349 | 918 | 919 |
| GPU seconds to t_end | 9.17 | 11.57 | 176.3 | 229.6 |
| field L1 rho / rho0, r < 1.5 R_s | 0.0629 | 0.0648 | 0.0369 | 0.0377 |
| field L1 v_r / max v_r | 0.0207 | 0.0210 | 0.0106 | 0.0107 |
| field L1 P / max P | 0.0164 | 0.0170 | 0.0096 | 0.0099 |
| peak B/cell (views) | 2298 | 2319 | 2298 | 2319 |
| GPU MB in use, of 42405 | 6388 | 6436 | 40125 | 40484 |

Whole step 21-23 % fewer cell updates/s, GPU time to t_end +26-30 %. Sedov gets no accuracy for it: field L1 1.5-4 % worse.
Field L1: `scripts/sedov_field_l1.py`, the cells' piecewise-constant field on a fixed lattice, never at generators.
Mesh cost is the same: one mesh update per step. All of the cost is the second flux and update sweep.

Sides that fell back to the cell's state rise about 8x (256^3: 198,081 to 1,488,593, of 2.7e8 per step), from the whole-step
extrapolation of the second pass. The 256^3 two-pass run had `stuck=6` in the mesh repair (one pass 0), `host_flips=0`, no
rebuild: not looked into. Mass and energy conserved to 2e-16 in all runs.

## Memory

256^3 two passes fit: 2319 B/cell, 40484 of 42405 MB in use, 1921 MB spare (2280 MB before).

The naive port keeps a copy of W, g and the old centroid, 184 B/cell = 3087 MB, which does not fit. So the cell rows moved:
with two passes they live in an array of their own (`held_`, 176 B/cell of double, read after the mesh update, no copy
kernel) and the mesh's scratch holds only the fluxes (318 B/cell instead of 488), plus 24 B/cell for the old centroids. Net
+21 B/cell: 355 MB at 256^3. These arrays exist only when two passes are selected.

| Moving 256^3 (40 steps), 2 passes | B/cell | GPU MB in use | fits | M cell updates/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| double | 2319 | 40484 | yes | 70.1 at 1305 MHz |
| `--precision=mixed` | 2445 (measured at 128^3, 240^3) | about 42600 | **no**, allocation fails at `hydro_old_centroid` | |
| `--precision=grad32` | 2255 | 39410 | yes | 69.3 at 1095 MHz |
| `--precision=flux32` | 2246 | 39261 | yes | 63.3 at 1095 MHz |
| `--update=cell` | 2246 | 39261 | yes | 31.3 at 1095 MHz |

Mixed one pass still fits (2334 B/cell, 40727 MB, 918 steps, 188.9 s at 1095 MHz). Mixed two passes does not because the
scratch cannot shrink below the mesh's own spare ring pool, 244 B/cell, while mixed's flux needs only 159, and its float face
rows sit beside the double arrays. It lacks about 200 MB. What could be dropped: mixed's double face area and centroid
arrays (in voronoi-cpp); or take `grad32`, which saves more than mixed here. At 240^3 mixed two passes runs (65.5 M/s, 35377 MB).

## Tests

The moving tests of `hydro` run with both integrators. No expectation had to change: under two passes each still passes its
old bound (uniform 5e-16, conserved 6e-18, Galilean 7e-8, mesh exact, same bits). The new gate `hydro_wave`, bounds 10 %
above measured values, the same to 3 digits on CPU and A100 (GPU job 31925198):

| | L1 rho, nx 24, 48 | rate |
|---|---|---|
| vortex, jitter 0.1, t = 2, one pass | 1.73e-3, 8.06e-4 | 1.11 |
| vortex, two passes | 2.37e-3, 5.17e-4 | 2.20 |
| sound, amp 1e-3, jitter 0.25, one pass | 6.49e-5, 6.62e-5 | -0.03 |
| sound, two passes | 2.44e-5, 7.37e-6 | 1.73 |

The sound gate is at jitter 0.25 and not 0.1: at 0.1 the two integrators agree to 3 digits, so the gate would tell them apart at
no point. Reproduced here: the floor of one pass in rho under regularised, non-rigid mesh motion, diagnosis note 23.
