# Stage 2D: hydro, Kelvin-Helmholtz (2026-11-05)

Measured on voronoi-cpp branch dim-2d, before the split; ported here by diff, see "The port" at the end. Two flux
computations there were an option of their own (`--two-flux`, order 1, per-face, 2D); here they are the integrator of
research_notes/two_pass_2026-11-05.md, the default on moving meshes at order 1 as in 3D. "One flux" and "two fluxes" below
are `--integrator=one-pass` and `two-pass`. The tables below were measured with the code of dim-2d.

The mesh's part of this stage is in voronoi-cpp, `research_notes/mesh_2d_2026-11-05.md`.

## Hydro

FiniteVolume<2> is the 3D code; new: StepRule::luna (a host branch, kernel only in 2D). Faces of zero size carry no flux, in
every dimension and in both integrators (issue #6).

Measured AREPO-ng difference: AREPO-ng makes two flux computations a step (run.cc:165, 284): half dt on the old mesh without
time extrapolation, half dt on the new one with W and gradients of the start, advanced by the convective derivative
(mesh_fluxes.cc:601-740). Ours (3D and 2D default) is one flux on the old mesh, MUSCL-Hancock. `--two-flux` in hydro_kh2d
implemented theirs for 2D, order 1, per-face; `Integrator::two_pass` is that step here.

## KH

LUNA's pipeline reproduced: LUNA's M errors in this job equal its test's recorded values to the digit printed.
Our static square lattice matches LUNA MC within 10 % at the same steps.

Why the moving mesh loses at the same N without bulk (N = 256, job 31919802): not time (max|e| 0.021 at Courant 0.8, 0.024 at
0.1: spatial), not the limiter (N = 128: 0.13 with and without), not one flux against two (N = 128: 0.135 against 0.130).
A static jittered mesh (0.2) already has max|e| 0.011 against 0.0042 on the lattice: the loss is the irregular mesh itself. The KH
setup on a lattice aligned with the flow is the best case for a fixed grid.

Later measurement corrects the N = 128 reading above: two fluxes do nothing at N <= 512, and decide at N >= 1024 (below).
