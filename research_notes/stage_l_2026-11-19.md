# Stage L alone: a long-time exact test for luna-cpp #30 (2026-11-19)

Branch stage-l (from main 283fee3). [M] measured, [I] inference. Context: research_notes/super_tube_v2_2026-11-18.md; #30 (the moving mesh
stalls in the tube v2's stage L).

## The test

Stage L of the tube v2 alone: one Riemann problem, (ρ, v, P) = (1, 0, 0.1) next to (r, 0, 1e-7 r) at x = 0, γ = 1.4, open walls, on
[-2, 9], to t = 4. Three density ratios, the pressure ratio 1e6 times the density ratio:
- r = 1e-3: v1's stage E;
- r = 1e-6: the tube's stage L;
- r = 1e-9.

The problems are `stage-l-3`, `-6` and `-9` in `problems_1d.hpp` and `scripts/problems_1d.py`.

Exact waves (`scripts/stage_l.py check`, the bisection solver with vacuum handling, pressures down to 1e-30) [M]:

| ratio | p* | u* | ρ* left, right | left wave | right shock | span at t = 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e3 | 9.8e-4 | 0.904 | 0.0368, 0.006 | rarefaction −0.374 to 0.711 | 1.085 | [−1.50, 4.34] |
| 1e6 | 2.6e-6 | 1.458 | 5.2e-4, 6e-6 | −0.374 to 1.375 | 1.750 | [−1.50, 7.00] |
| 1e9 | 3.5e-9 | 1.710 | 4.7e-6, 6e-9 | −0.374 to 1.678 | 2.052 | [−1.50, 8.21] |

No wave reaches a wall by t = 4, so the problem is self-similar and exact at every time. (On [−2, 8] the 1e9 shock would have reached the
wall; its speed is (γ+1)/2 u*, faster than the 1e6 one.) At N = 1024, h₀ = 11/1024 = 0.0107, about the tube's spacing at N = 4096.

Runs (`scripts/stage_l_run.sh`, `stage_l.py score`):
- LUNA with its floors on, at t = 0.1, 0.5, 0.81, 2 and 4;
- luna-cpp static and moving, one run each to t = 4, with snapshots at 18 times (`hydro_1d --times`, new: it reads the state and changes
  nothing);
- N = 1024 to 32768, each ratio.

CPU: one core per run on general. Moving N ≥ 8192 on one A100. Tables: reports/stage_l.md.

## Accuracy [M]

L1(ρ) against the exact solution, t = 4 (t = 0.1 in reports/stage_l.md):

| ratio | series | 1024 | 4096 | 32768 | order 16384 → 32768 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e3 | LUNA = static | 6.03e-3 | 1.41e-3 | 1.84e-4 | 1.02 |
| 1e3 | moving | 4.66e-3 | 1.57e-3 | 1.97e-4 | 0.99 |
| 1e6 | LUNA = static | 4.56e-3 | 1.17e-3 | 2.58e-4 | 0.63 |
| 1e6 | moving | 3.02e-3 | 9.57e-4 | 1.51e-4 | 0.99 |
| 1e9 | LUNA = static | 4.54e-3 | 1.08e-3 | 1.42e-4 | 1.00 |
| 1e9 | moving | stops at t = 0.015 | stops at t = 3.86 | stops at t = 3.73 | - |

- Static luna-cpp equals LUNA with its floors to the digits shown, at every ratio, N and time.
- All converge at first order, as a contact problem should.
- Moving is 1.2 to 1.7 times better than static at 1e6, and within 25 % at 1e3 (better at 1024, worse at 4096 and 32768).
- At 1e6, static's order falls to 0.6 by t = 4. At 1e6 and 1e9, static and LUNA spread the expansion front into the near-vacuum gas all the
  way to the wall, with ρ ~ 1e-5 where the exact solution has 1e-6 to 1e-9 (figure `stage_l_profiles`). L1 barely sees this, since the
  density there is tiny.

## The pile-up: none in stage L alone [M]

Moving, at all 18 snapshot times (`stage_l_pileup`, reports/stage_l.md):
- The smallest inner cell stays at 0.08 to 0.17 h₀, at every ratio and N from 1024 to 32768.
- Generators within h₀ of the exact contact: at most 17, at t ≤ 0.3 while the contact is young (N = 1024 to 4096); later 0 or 1,
  except up to 7 at 1e3, N = 1024, around t = 2.

So **stage L alone does not reproduce #30** at 1e3 or 1e6 at any N to t = 4; the 1e6 moving run reaches t = 4 in 11,163 steps at 1024.
The 1e9 stops are a different failure:
- At N = 1024 the near-vacuum gas at the open right wall (ρ = 1e-9, P = 1e-16) is heated to P = 3e-3 and driven out at u ≈ 1540 by
  t = 0.015. The end cell's generator sits at the wall with V = 3e-9 h₀.
- At N = 2048 to 32768 the runs reach t = 3.7 to 3.9. There, fast near-vacuum gas ahead of the shock (u ≈ 2.1) reaches the open wall, and
  the end cell collapses there (#14).
- At t = 3.5 the smallest cells are 0.1 h₀ at the shock; nothing has piled up at the contact.

## Where #30 comes from [M] and why [I]

The tube's moving run (m0, N = 4096, A100, snapshots every 0.05; `hydro_1d --times`) shows when it starts:

| t | steps | smallest cell / h | cells within h of it | where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.15 | 7,798 | 8.9e-3 | 50 | x = 13.19 (E) |
| 0.20 | 18,135 | 2.7e-3 | 98 | x = 15.31, ρ = 1e-4, P = 1.1e-3 |
| 0.25 | 948,194 | 9.9e-5 | 101 | x = 15.27, uniform P = 0.085, u = −0.166 |
| 0.30 | 3,343,002 | 5.5e-5 | 102 | x = 15.25 |
| 0.60 | 40,133,002 | 2.3e-5 | 104 | x = 15.10, uniform P = 0.366, u = −0.469 |

From t = 0.2 on, about 100 generators lie in a layer about 1e-5 wide.
- That is the number of cells of stage L's 10⁻⁶ slab (1.0 wide, 102 cells at h = 0.0098).
- At N = 32768 the run reaches t = 0.646 in 24 h. There 809 cells lie within 2e-5 of x = 15.0755, against about 814 cells in the slab at
  that spacing.
- The layer is a contact: P and u are uniform across it, ρ runs 0.03 to 0.6.

The slab is squeezed from both sides. L's own shock runs right into it, and the shock that the ambient gas beyond the slab (P = 1) drives
left into it at −5.5 meets L's at t = 0.137. A slab of mass 1e-6 compressed to ρ ~ 0.1 to 1 is physically only 1e-6 to 1e-5 wide.

The moving mesh keeps every generator, so each of the slab's ~100 cells (~800 at 32768) shrinks to about 1e-7 to 1e-9, and the time step
with them. Stage L alone compresses the slab by one shock only (×6), so there is no squeeze and no pile-up. [I] This is #25's squeezed
layer (stage E, 1e-3), with the slab's mass 1000 times smaller. #25's fix (interpolating the point velocity) keeps the points ordered, but
it cannot avoid the physical compression. A cure needs fewer cells in a layer of tiny mass: derefinement, merging cells below a mass or
size threshold, as AREPO does.

Reduced reproducer [M]: `super-v2-l` (stage L's window of the tube: the high state, the slab, then the ambient gas; 256 cells, the
spacing of N = 4096; open walls), moving, to t = 0.35:
- the steps per interval jump at t = 0.17 to 0.2;
- 8.9 million steps to t = 0.35, against 1,385 to t = 0.15;
- the CTest `hydro1d_issue30` holds it, with the bound "t = 0.35 within 100,000 steps, smallest cell > 1e-3 h". Today it reaches only
  t = 0.2 in 100,000 steps, smallest cell 3.3e-4 h, so the test is DISABLED until #30 is fixed (`ctest -R issue30` lists it).
