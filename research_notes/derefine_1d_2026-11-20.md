# Derefinement in 1D: removing cells that a squeezed layer makes too small (luna-cpp #30, 2026-11-20)

Branch deref-1d (from main 066886a, voronoi-cpp 1741a1a). [M] measured, [I] inference. Context: #30, research_notes/stage_l_2026-11-19.md
(the diagnosis), research_notes/super_tube_v2_2026-11-18.md (the tube).

## What it does

`FiniteVolume<1>::set_derefine(V_min)` (moving mesh only; 0, the default, is off). After each step, once the generators are sorted and
any inflow cells are supplied, every cell with Voronoi length below V_min loses its generator (`derefine()`, finite_volume_1d.hpp):

1. find the cells below V_min (one scan over the sorted line);
2. `voronoi::Mesh<1>::remove(ids)` takes their generators off the line. It defers a cell whose neighbour is going in the same call,
   which the next step takes, and it keeps at least 3 cells. It reports, per removed cell, its two neighbours and the length
   each one takes over (half the gap to the removed generator on that side; the two add up to the removed cell's length);
3. the removed cell's mass, momentum and energy go to the two neighbours in those proportions. This is the exact conservative
   remap: removing a generator only enlarges its neighbours, and each takes the part of the old cell it now covers. A wall on one
   side gets nothing, and the inner neighbour takes all;
4. the last ids fill the holes (`Changes::moved`): conserved totals and positions are copied, and the cell count drops;
5. in the fused step the next dt is computed again, as after an insertion.

`hydro_1d --derefine=f` sets V_min = f L / N, a fraction of the initial spacing h.

**The neighbour.** Neither "the smaller neighbour" nor "the one across the contact" is chosen: both neighbours share the cell in
proportion to the length they take over, as in AREPO, where a removed cell's conserved quantities go to all its Voronoi neighbours
in proportion to the volume each gains (Springel 2010, MNRAS 401, 791, on derefinement). That is the only split that is exact
for a uniform state (a uniform cell stays uniform: unit test), and it needs no side to be picked. The cost is mixing across
whatever the small cell sits between. In #30 the small cells lie inside the squeezed slab, both of whose neighbours are slab gas,
except at its two edges [I].

**Order of the sums.** Two removed cells at slots s - 1 and s + 1 both give to the cell at s. The shares are added in two passes,
first every removed cell's share to its left neighbour, then to its right one. Within a pass no two removed cells share a
neighbour, so there are no atomics and the bits are the same on every run and backend schedule.

**Neutral when nothing triggers.** If no cell is below V_min, `derefine()` scans the cells and returns, changing nothing. Unit test:
Sod moving at f = 1e-3 removes no cell and gives the same bits as without derefinement. The ladder below checks the same for all
30 problems.

## Refinement (2026-11-22)

`FiniteVolume<1>::set_refine(V_max, linear)` with the driver flags `hydro_1d --refine=F [--split=linear]` (V_max = F h). It runs after
derefinement in each moving step and is off by default. voronoi-cpp's `Mesh<1>::insert` already existed (the inflow walls use it), so
the library needed nothing new.

- **Where the new generator goes.** The midpoint of the cell's larger gap, between its generator and the farther neighbour generator.
  If that side is a wall, the point goes halfway to the wall. The two gaps there become equal. Every cell the split leaves (the new
  one, the parent and the neighbour across the gap) is at least a quarter of the split cell's length, so the mesh stays regular. The
  centre of mass does not work: in a regular cell it is the generator itself.
- **The remap.** In 1D the new cell takes length from two old cells: the parent and the neighbour across the gap. `Mesh<1>::insert`
  reports both lengths. The new cell gets what lies on them and the old cells keep the rest, so the totals are kept by construction.
  - constant split: the fraction overlap / volume of each old cell's totals;
  - linear split: each old cell's minmod-limited linear profile of the conserved densities, integrated over its piece (slope 0 next to
    a wall).

  A uniform state has slope 0, so it stays uniform with either split.
- **Bits.** Three passes (the pieces, then the old cells left of the new ones, then those right of them) and no atomics. When no cell
  crosses V_max, nothing changes.
- **Hysteresis.** Since every cell a split leaves is at least V/4, refinement and derefinement together need V_max > 4 V_min, and
  `set_refine` throws otherwise. Here F = 2 with f = 3e-2: a factor of 67.

**Unit tests [M].** On a jittered periodic line, 21 cells are added over 20 steps with each split. The totals are kept to 7e-18 to
3e-17, and a uniform state stays uniform to 4e-14. Sod with V_max = 20 h adds no cell and has the same bits as without refinement.
V_max = h with V_min = 0.3 h is refused.
