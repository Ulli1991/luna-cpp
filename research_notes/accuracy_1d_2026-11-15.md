# 1D accuracy against LUNA: #25 narrowed, Noh, the post-shock trail (2026-11-15)

Branch accuracy-1d (from main e350dd7 plus fix-25). [M] measured, [I] inference. CPU: OpenMP build, one thread on the login node or
18 threads on general; GPU: A100. L1 in LUNA's norm (scripts/ladder_1d.py). #23's ladder: luna-cpp-fix23/build-ladder/build-1dtests.

## #25: interpolate only where the generator is far from its centroid [M]

fix-25's cost is cell-to-cell density noise behind shocks: Toro 5 at 131072, between the contact and the right shock, rho alternates
by 6e-3 (2e-4 of 31.04) against 1e-5 at #23; the velocities and pressures are smooth, so it is entropy noise left in the cells. A
cell that a shock compresses is lopsided while it passes (c / r up to (1 - 1/k) / (1 + 1/k) at compression k, 0.71 for k = 6), and
the interpolation then mixes the post-shock velocity into the next point.

Now the interpolation's weight rises from 0 at |c| = 0.75 r to 1 at r, where AREPO-ng's drift starts and saturates. Below 0.75 r the
point velocity is the cell's mean plus the drift, #23's arithmetic. Measured, CPU:

| run | off (#23) | fix-25 | from 0.75 r | from 0.9 r |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stage E alone, 370 cells (spacing of 4096), to t = 0.2: smallest cell | 3.5e-7 h, stall at t = 0.132 | 8.4e-3 h | 5.4e-3 h | 4.1e-3 h |
| stage E alone, 2960 cells (spacing of 32768), to t = 0.2: smallest cell | 2.5e-3 h | - | 6.0e-3 h | 1.6e-3 h |
| Shu-Osher 65536, L1 rho | 7.064e-4 | 8.630e-4 | 7.064e-4 | 7.064e-4 |
| shock-10 65536, L1 rho | 4.396e-5 | 9.704e-5 | - | 4.396e-5 |
| Toro 3 16384, L1 rho | 1.429e-4 | 1.778e-4 | 1.473e-4 | - |

Stage E alone at the spacing of 32768 does not collapse even at the mean velocity, so the old regression test (2960 cells to
t = 0.125, 4.8e-3 h without the fix against a bound of 5e-3 h) did not separate the two; the test now runs 370 cells to t = 0.2.

The tube at N = 4096 reaches t = 2 (A100, 6.55 million steps, 683 s; fix-25 4.96 million): L1 rho over all stages 1.154, static 2.663,
fix-25 1.001.

## Noh (#27) [M]

Cause of the failure: AREPO's time step had no pair term at a wall. Cold gas at v = -1 into a reflecting wall has c ~ 1e-3 and
|v - w| = 0 on the moving mesh, so dt = r / (c + |v - w|) is huge and the first step was the whole run (0.6). The wall's term is now
that of the end cell's ghost at its generator's mirror (the stencil's ghost). Moving Noh then runs at every N tried, AREPO's rule at
0.4: L1 rho 4.91e-3 (256), 1.15e-3 (1024), 6.98e-4 (2048), 5.30e-4 (4096); LUNA 9.55e-4 (1024), 5.54e-4 (2048), 2.69e-4 (4096).

What is left behind the shock: an odd-even density pattern of rms 1.3e-3 (3e-4 of 4) at uniform pressure, the same at every N, so it
is a floor (2e-4 of the 5.3e-4 at 4096). The rest is the shock region, first order as LUNA's.

## The post-shock trail on a moving mesh [M]

Pure shocks fall behind LUNA at high N for the same reason (shock-3 65536: 3.79e-5 against LUNA 1.71e-5; the error spread evenly
over the path of the shock, where LUNA's is 1e-7 per bin). In the frame of the cells HLLC's contact wave has no speed, so nothing damps
an entropy error once a cell has it. Tried, each against shock-3 at 4096 (L1 2.55e-4, trail rms 3.6e-4) and Noh:

| switch | trail | L1 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| HLL everywhere | 1.25e-5 | 2.32e-4 | not LUNA's solver; smears contacts |
| HLLC, a 5 % HLL blend everywhere | 1.8e-5 | 2.19e-4 | Toro 3 at 4096 2.6x worse (contacts) |
| HLL at compressive faces with a pressure jump > 10-30 % | 1.1e-3 | 3.1e-4 | the switching makes noise |
| smooth HLL blend at shocks (pressure jump over 4 cells) | 3.4e-4 to 2.5e-3 | 2.9e-4 to 3.9e-4 | worse |
| minmod at shock cells (neighbours' pressures differ by > 10 %) | 1.5e-4 | 3.2e-4 | shock-3 65536 3.79e-5 -> 3.11e-5, but shock-10 65536 4.40e-5 -> 6.38e-5, Toro 5 32768 4.53e-4 -> 5.16e-4 |
| minmod everywhere (Noh) | rms 2e-5 | Noh 4096 3.98e-4 | LUNA's MC is the like-for-like limiter |

None is kept. Open: a dissipation that acts on entropy errors of a few 1e-4 but not on contacts.
